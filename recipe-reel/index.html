      Filming tips

      • Good lightFilm near a window. Daylight makes food look its best.
      • Wipe the lensA quick wipe on your shirt makes everything sharper.
      • Film tall for Shorts, TikTok and ReelsHold your iPad or phone upright. Wide clips work too — we fit them in.
      • One clip per stepStart filming, do the step, stop. Hold still and get close to your hands.
      • Long clips are fineWe trim and speed them up to fit the length you pick.
      • Photos work tooWe add a slow zoom so they don’t look still.
