How long does delivery take?

Orders usually arrive in 10–20 business days. Regional and remote areas can take a few days longer. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Is shipping free?

Yes. Shipping is free on every order within Australia.

Will it arrive before Christmas?

Order by 1 December for the best chance. We can't promise exact dates in the Christmas rush, but you'll always get your tracking number.

Where do your products ship from?

We work with overseas partner warehouses to keep our prices low, which is why delivery takes a bit longer than buying in a shop.

Will the Pop Shade fit my car?

It opens to about 140 × 80 cm, which fits most cars, hatches and small SUVs. Please measure your windscreen first. Large 4WDs and utes may need a bigger size.

What size is the Chill Pad?

About 70 × 55 cm, which suits most cats and small-to-medium dogs.

Something's wrong with my order. What do I do?

Email us with your order number and a photo and we'll make it right with a replacement or a refund. See our Refund Policy for details.
