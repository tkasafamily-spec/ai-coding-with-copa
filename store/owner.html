How to use it: put an item in your AliExpress cart (delivering to your own address) and go to checkout. Take the total in AUD, including shipping and GST, and type it into "AliExpress cost". Ad cost: start at $0 while you post your own TikToks and Reels. If you run paid ads, divide what you spent by how many sales you got.

Rules of thumb

  • Keep profit per sale above $15 with no ads. That leaves room to pay for ads later.
  • If paid ads cost more per sale than your profit, stop the ad, not the product. Try a new video first.
  • The Summer Pack bundle makes the most money per customer, so push it in your videos.
  • Check AliExpress prices every week. Suppliers change prices and run out of stock.
