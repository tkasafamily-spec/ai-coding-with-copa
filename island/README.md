# Sunset Key

A small open-world island game for iPad: steal cars, rob people and shops, and outrun the cops.

Play it at **https://tkasafamily-spec.github.io/ai-coding-with-copa/island/** (in Safari).
Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** so it opens full screen and keeps working with no internet.

- **Left thumb:** drag to walk (push far to run) or drive.
- **Right thumb:** drag to look around.
- **🚗** get in or out of cars. **👊** rob, punch, and hold it at the glowing $ rings to rob shops and the bank.
- **Stars** show how hard the cops chase you. Hide until they run out, or drive into the pink Spray Shack ($200).
- Keyboard works too: WASD, Shift to run, E car, R rob, Space jump/handbrake, H horn.

Your money is saved on the device. Everything is drawn by `game.js`; `three.module.min.js` is the 3D library (three.js r170).
