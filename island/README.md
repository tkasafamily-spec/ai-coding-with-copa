# Sunset Key

A small open-world island game for iPad: steal cars, rob people and shops, do missions, and outrun the cops.

Play it at **https://tkasafamily-spec.github.io/ai-coding-with-copa/island/** (in Safari).
Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** so it opens full screen and keeps working with no internet.

## What's in it
- A city, beaches, a pier and a lighthouse, with a day-night cycle, real shadows, lit windows and street lights at night.
- Cars, motorbikes, speedboats and jet skis. You can swim, too (the cops can't follow you into the sea).
- Rob people, 4 shops and the bank. Buy a pistol, SMG and armor at the GUN SHOP.
- Cops: stars, police cars, cops on foot, tasers at 2 stars, guns at 3+, a helicopter at 4+.
- 6 story missions (the yellow **M** on the map) with cash rewards.
- A car radio with 3 stations (📻 button), made in code.

## Controls
- **Left thumb:** drag to walk (push far to run), swim or drive. **Right thumb:** drag to look around.
- **🚗** in/out of vehicles. **👊/🔫** rob, punch or shoot (hold at the glowing $ rings to rob shops).
- Tap the weapon box under your money to switch weapons.
- Keyboard: WASD, Shift run, E vehicle, R rob/shoot, G switch weapon, T radio, Space jump/handbrake, H horn.

If it runs slowly, pause (❚❚) and switch **Graphics** to FAST.

Your money, guns and mission progress are saved on the device. `game.js` is the game, `radio.js` makes the music,
and `three.module.min.js` is the 3D library (three.js r170).
