How one Short gets made

About an hour per Short once you've done it twice. All of these tools have free plans.

Get a script

Pick one of the ready scripts below, or make a new one with the Script Maker and Claude.

Tool: Claude

Make the cast pictures

One time only. Make one picture of each character and save them. You'll reuse them in every Short so everyone looks the same.

Tool: Google Flow (image) or Gemini. Prompts are in Cast.

Make the clips

In Google Flow, start a project, set it to portrait 9:16, use Ingredients to Video, add the cast pictures for that scene, paste the scene prompt. Each scene = one 8-second clip. Make 2 of each and keep the best.

Tool: labs.google/flow

Make the voice

Paste the voiceover. Easiest: one narrator voice for everything. Better: a different voice for each character. Download the audio.

Tool: ElevenLabs, or CapCut's free text-to-speech

Edit it

CapCut, 9:16. Clips in order, voice on top, trim each clip to its lines. Auto captions, big and bold in the middle. A sound effect on every action. Music quiet underneath.

Tool: CapCut (phone or iPad)

Upload

Go through the upload checklist. Post at the same time every day.

Tool: YouTube app

What makes a Short go viral

    
YouTube shows your Short to a few people first. If they keep watching instead of swiping, it shows it to more. These rules are all about stopping the swipe.

    

      
Hook in 1 second
Start in the middle of the action or with the big offer. No "hi guys", no intro, no slow start. The first frame should already be interesting.

      
Change every 2–3 seconds
New shot, new problem, new joke. If nothing changes for 4 seconds, people swipe.

      
Stakes go up 3 times
Easy → uh-oh → disaster → twist. Each scene should make it worse or crazier.

      
Twist ending
Never end how people expect. Quack gets the money. The coins are chocolate. Granny wins.

      
Make it loop
If the last line leads back into the first scene, people watch twice without noticing. That counts as more watch time.

      
Same characters, every time
When people know Pip, Mo and Quack, they come back for them. That's how you get subscribers, not just views.

      
Big captions
2–4 words at a time, in the middle of the screen, with the key word in yellow. Lots of people watch with the sound off.

      
Sound on every action
Whoosh, boing, splash, squeak. Sound effects make cheap animation feel expensive.

      
40–55 seconds
Long enough for a story, short enough that most people finish it.

    

    

      
Hook bank

      
Swap in your own words. Use one as the first line or the on-screen text.

      
    
        
  • "[Rich guy] said: [do something simple], win [huge money]."

    •

    • 
        
  • "Would you [scary thing] for [money]?"

  • "Nobody expected [weakest character] to win."
    •

After you post

      
Reply to every comment in the first hour. After a day, open YouTube Studio → the Short → Viewed vs. swiped away. Above 70% viewed is great. If it's low, the first second wasn't strong enough: make the next hook crazier. Make more of whatever gets the most views.
