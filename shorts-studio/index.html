What makes a facts Short go viral

    

      
Lead with the shock
The first line is the craziest part: "This man sold 10% of Apple for $800." Explain after.

      
A number on screen
Big counting numbers ($800, $50 million, 9 a day) stop thumbs. Every Short should have at least one.

      
New picture every 2–3 seconds
One beat = one sentence = one picture. Never hold a picture for more than 4 seconds.

      
Then vs. now
Tiny start, huge ending. "From 9 drinks a day… to 2 billion." People love the contrast.

      
End with a question
"Would you have sold it?" makes people comment, and comments push the Short to more people.

      
Only true facts
Facts channels die when people catch mistakes in the comments. Check every number. Keep the sources.

      
20–30 seconds
Short enough that people watch to the end and replay it. That's what YouTube rewards most.

      
Post every day
Same time every day. A series like "$1 decisions that changed history" makes people subscribe.
